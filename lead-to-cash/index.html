Overview This case study describes the end-to-end lead-to-cash process for creating a new subscription in a landscape that combines Salesforce (CRM, CPQ and Service), Oracle E-Business Suite (Order Management and Receivables), SAP BRIM (billing and usage rating), a custom Subscription and Billing system (SNB) and AWS-metered usage. An integration layer orchestrates the hand-offs between these platforms using REST and SOAP APIs, so that a quote accepted in Salesforce results in an active subscription, an invoice and a support entitlement, with invoice details synchronised back to SNB and Salesforce. The document covers the master flow, the quote entry channels and order variants (new, add-on, expansion, upgrade and renewal), the integration contracts between systems, and fully worked greenfield and brownfield examples. The brownfield example converts a perpetual license with an active support and subscription (SNS) contract into a subscription and credits the unused SNS period. 2. Process stages and integration contracts # Stage System What happens 1 Lead and opportunity Salesforce Lead is qualified and converted to an opportunity. Entry can be sales-led or through self-service channels of the Subscription Commerce Cloud Platform. 2 Quote Salesforce CPQ, Deal Desk AI, PIM Offers and prices come from PIM. Deal Desk AI assists with configuration and discount guidance. Quote is priced and validated. 3 Credit check Credit service Runs before the quote is submitted for approval or accepted. A failed check blocks submission or routes the quote to finance review. 4 Order Oracle EBS OM Approved quote is created as a sales order and booked. 5 Subscription SNB Order creates the subscription ID (SID), activates it, provisions entitlements and sends a notification email. 6 Customer onboarding SNB Customer selects payment method and bill-to address. 7 Billing and usage SAP BRIM Recurring charges and AWS usage are rated and billed. Order data supports billing and invoice. 8 Invoice Oracle EBS AR Invoice is generated and posted. Invoice details are synchronised to SNB and Salesforce. 9 Support Salesforce Service Support entitlement is created and linked to the SID and IB instance.
