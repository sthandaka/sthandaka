Lead-to-Cash: Subscription Creation Across Salesforce, Oracle EBS, SAP BRIM and SNB

Integration architecture, process flows and worked examples for a multi-ERP subscription commerce platform

1. Overview

This case study describes the end-to-end lead-to-cash process for creating a new subscription in a landscape that combines Salesforce (CRM, CPQ and Service), Oracle E-Business Suite (Order Management and Receivables), SAP BRIM (billing and usage rating), a custom Subscription and Billing system (SNB) and AWS-metered usage. An integration layer orchestrates the hand-offs between these platforms using REST and SOAP APIs, so that a quote accepted in Salesforce results in an active subscription, an invoice and a support entitlement, with invoice details synchronised back to SNB and Salesforce.

The document covers the master flow, the quote entry channels and order variants (new, add-on, expansion, upgrade and renewal), the integration contracts between systems, and fully worked greenfield and brownfield examples. The brownfield example converts a perpetual license with an active support and subscription (SNS) contract into a subscription and credits the unused SNS period.

8. Use cases: quote and order variants on an existing subscription

Each example is independent and starts from the same baseline: SUB-100245, 100 units at $1,200/yr per unit ($10,000/mo), term 2026-02-10 to 2029-02-09, billed monthly on the 1st.

A. Add-on: additional quantity of existing products

Customer adds 10 units mid-term. The change is co-termed to the existing end date and the first partial month is prorated.

B. Expansion: new product on an existing subscription

Customer adds a new product line. It is provisioned against the existing IB instance and co-termed.

C. Upgrade: Standard to Enterprise

Edition changes mid-cycle on the same SID. Unused Standard value for the remaining 16 days is credited and Enterprise is charged for the same days.

D. Renewal: auto and manual

Renewal starts 60 days before term end. The path differs only in who creates and accepts the quote.

Daily proration uses the actual days in the month. Renewal uplift of 5% is an illustrative pricing rule.

9. Assumptions and notes

1. All identifiers (quote, order, subscription, invoice, entitlement, IB instance), dates, customer names and amounts are fictitious and for illustration only. No proprietary data is included.

2. SNB denotes the custom Subscription and Billing system. SNS denotes the support and subscription contract associated with perpetual licenses. SS denotes self-service.

3. Integration protocols follow common patterns for each platform (REST/JSON with OAuth 2.0; SOAP with WS-Security for Oracle EBS and SAP). The specific services and endpoints in a production landscape will differ.

4. Credit is calculated on actual days remaining over a 365-day year. Proration for monthly cycles uses actual days in the month.

5. Renewal lead time of 60 days, the 5% uplift and the auto-renewal booking at T-30 days are example business rules.

All identifiers, dates and amounts in this case study are fictitious sample values. Integration protocols shown are typical industry patterns.
